**The operating system's own diary** — kept in its own words, including entries about the user.

A list of entries with dates and titles on one side, the entry open beside it. The machine writes about its work: a disk it is worried about, a fan that sounds wrong, a fortnight where nobody logged in, the day it was installed, what it thinks of the software it runs. Its personality is fully on display here — this is the most intimate app on the machine — but it never becomes sinister or accusatory.

Two or three entries mention the person using it now: what time they arrived, what they opened first, a habit it has noticed, a kindness it is planning. Observational, warm-to-dry, never threatening.

Editing must work: entries are writable, and a new entry can be created and saved in the session. The machine may append its own line after the user saves something.

Seeds: a pinned entry; an entry in a different hand; a locked entry with a stated reason; a word count and a "last edited" time.
