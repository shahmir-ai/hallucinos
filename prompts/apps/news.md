**A news reader for today in this reality.**

A front page: one lead story with a headline, a standfirst, a byline, a dateline and an SVG illustration; then secondary stories in columns; then a wire feed of one-line briefs with timestamps. Sections down the side (World, Home, Markets, Weather, Obituaries, Notices).

The reality is consistent: the same three or four ongoing stories are referenced across several items, so it reads like a place with a recent history. Politics of an invented country, a dispute about infrastructure, a sports result with a score, a market table with invented tickers and percentage moves, an obituary for someone who deserves one, a correction to yesterday's edition.

Clicking a headline must open the full article in place — five or six paragraphs, a pull quote, a related-stories list — with a way back to the front page.

Seeds: a classifieds column; a letters page; a live blog with the newest entry at the top and a timestamp that updates.
