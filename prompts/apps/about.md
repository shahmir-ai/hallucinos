**About this computer**: the operating system's own history, its specifications, and its credits.

Specifications, given exactly: processor with a model name and clock, memory installed and how much is addressable, storage devices by invented manufacturer with capacities and a health figure, display resolution and refresh, serial number, machine name, the date it was built and where, the date it was last serviced and by whom, uptime.

History: three to six dated milestones — first release, the version that was withdrawn, the year it was ported to something unlikely, who bought whom, the release you are now looking at. It should read like an institution with a past.

Credits: the people who made it, by full name, with roles that start ordinary and get narrower until one of them is a single word, one is a span of years with no explanation, and the last one is not a job at all. A dedication is welcome.

Seeds: a legal notices pane in small type with one real-sounding but invented licence; a system report that can be "copied"; a registration card with the owner's name filled in; an easter egg reached by clicking the version number several times.
