**Forecasts for places that do not exist, with warnings.**

A current conditions panel — temperature, an SVG sky drawing, "feels like", wind with direction, humidity, pressure with a trend arrow, visibility, sunrise and sunset to the minute — then hourly for the next twelve hours and daily for the next week, each with high, low, an icon you draw, and a precipitation chance.

The warnings are where the machine shows its personality: a banner with a category, an issuing authority, a valid-from and valid-until, and instructions. One or two warnings are the ordinary kind that any forecast carries; one is a hazard only this world has, named in the vocabulary of the place this machine was built for, and issued with the same bureaucratic seriousness as the rest. Stated deadpan, never as a joke, never explained.

Seeds: a saved-locations list with wildly different climates; a radar map drawn as SVG blotches over a coastline; a marine or agricultural section with a different vocabulary; a note about which station this data came from and when it last reported.
